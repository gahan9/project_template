# Example - Reel from `<xyz-folder>`

Input: a workshop folder of stills and clips (stage, aisle walk, product
colors, group payoff). Goal: 9:16 brand promo, ~25s.

## ContentBrief: weekend-workshop

- Goal: reach
- Platforms: Instagram Reels
- Audience: 25-40 / layman
- Core promise: Go from zero to a stage song in one weekend session.
- Mode: offline-first hybrid
- Constraints: 1080x1920, identity lock, 20-35s, no face restyle

## Hooks

A (curiosity): POV: your first time on that stage.
B (pain-point): You keep saying you will learn. The weekend already started.
C (promise): Zero to a song in one workshop.

Recommended: A - motion into a real room beats a slogan in the first second.

## What this example refuses

- Beautifying faces on the group still.
- Morphing one ukulele color into another (that is a product identity change;
  use flash cuts between real clips instead).
- A generative crowd to "fill the room."
